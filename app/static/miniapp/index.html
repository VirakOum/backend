Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my ride?

You can pay conveniently with Cash on Boarding directly to your driver, or via Bakong KHQR upon pickup. No credit card required!

Where will the driver pick me up?

MyTravel provides door-to-door pickup. Simply enter your hotel or address note during booking, and the driver will confirm the location with you.

Can I track the driver on a live map?

Yes! Once booked, you can view driver status here, or tap "Open MyTravel App" to see turn-by-turn live GPS tracking on our native mobile app.
